Readings are estimates

Amphora reads data reported by your car's battery management system through a third-party OBD adapter and works some figures out from it. Readings can be inaccurate, incomplete or unavailable depending on your car, its software and your adapter. Don't rely on them alone when buying, selling, valuing or repairing a car, or for any safety decision. Amphora isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Mitsubishi, Hyundai or Kia.

Your car and adapter

Amphora only sends standard diagnostic read requests and doesn't change any settings in your car. You use an OBD adapter at your own risk: follow its maker's instructions, and unplug it when you're not using it if it could drain the 12 V battery. Never use the app while driving.
